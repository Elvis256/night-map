---
name: NightMap package model
description: NightMap's supply-first package strategy and payment approval constraint.
---

NightMap's goals are to maximize earnings and bring in as many users as possible, with a supply goal of at least 1,000 companions listed and ready to receive client requests. Keep basic listing and standard browsing free to reduce entry friction. The proposed model is High-End Access for clients at UGX 20,000/month and optional Featured placement for companions at UGX 100,000/week. Keep verification and safety features free, and do not promise that Featured placement guarantees bookings. Any separate VIP upsell must provide distinct value.

Avoid charging a fee on each booking or connection in the initial model; prioritize access subscriptions and optional visibility upgrades. Do not enable real checkout until a payment provider has approved NightMap's exact services and pricing structure in writing. Reducing payment-account freeze risk is a core business concern.

**Why:** The user prioritizes maximizing earnings and user growth while reducing avoidable payment-account freeze risk; evaluate options by net earning potential and acquisition potential, not risk avoidance alone.

**How to apply:** Keep package copy and future access controls consistent with this model. Treat the current package screen as a preview until checkout and entitlements are implemented; never imply that a payment succeeded when it did not.
